ℹ️ About Smart Emergency Helper

Smart Emergency Helper is a simple web-based emergency assistance tool designed to provide quick access to important emergency resources.

It helps users find nearby hospitals, police stations and pharmacies, view their location, save trusted contacts and access basic safety guidance.

Important: This website is an assistance tool and does not replace professional emergency services.
